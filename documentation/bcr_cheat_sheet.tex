\chapter{BCR (Benefit-Cost Ratio) Cheat Sheet}

Quick reference guide for all BCR perspectives calculated by CTCC.

\subsection*{Important Note on Revenue}
Revenue (rate-based revenue requirement) is a \textbf{transfer} from ratepayers to the utility/TSP, not a net social benefit. System/societal BCRs exclude revenue from total benefits. Revenue appears only in stakeholder BCRs where it's a benefit to utilities and a cost to ratepayers.

\subsection*{Cost categories}
\textbf{Capital} = build + ROW capital (acquisition + holding) + environmental mitigation. \textbf{Operational} = O\&M + insurance + ROW rent. \textbf{Energy/Emissions} = line losses + emissions + residual exceedance. Residual exceedance is a system/societal cost only. Rate base = AFUDC capital (build + row capital + env) at COD. Revenue is computed in real terms (Option A: real stream, real WACC).

\section{BCR Metrics (order matches Benefit-Cost Ratio Metrics tables)}

Order below matches the per-scenario and all-scenarios BCR tables in \texttt{results\_tables.tex} (updated by \texttt{papers/update\_results\_tables.py}).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_system}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / Total Costs

\textbf{Perspective:} Full societal/system-wide analysis

\textbf{Includes:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Benefits:} Congestion reduction + Curtailment reduction + Delivered energy (with conservative haircuts on congestion/curtailment; excludes revenue transfer)
\item \textbf{Costs:} Capital + Operational (O\&M + insurance only) + Energy/Emissions (line losses + emissions + residual exceedance) + Risk + Delay (everything)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Use Case:} Complete societal cost-benefit analysis including all externalities and risks.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_utility}}

\textbf{Equation:} Revenue / (Capital Costs + Delay Costs + Operational Costs). Benefits: Revenue only. Costs: Capital + Delay + Operational (O\&M + insurance only). Excludes: Line losses, Emissions, Residual exceedance, Risk.

\textbf{Key Insight:} Utilities don't receive congestion/curtailment benefits directly; they only get rate base recovery.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_ratepayer}}

\textbf{Equation:} (Congestion + Curtailment + Delivered energy Benefits) / (Revenue + Line Loss Costs). Benefits: Congestion + Curtailment + Delivered energy. Costs: Revenue + Line losses. Excludes: Capital/O\&M as separate line items (recovered via Revenue).

\textbf{Key Insight:} Ratepayers receive congestion, curtailment, and delivered-energy benefits but pay revenue/rate base and line losses through rates.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_capital}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / Capital Costs. Costs: Build + ROW + environmental only.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_capital\_and\_delay}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Capital Costs + Delay Costs). Costs: Capital + Delay (construction delay + congestion delay + curtailment delay).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_primary}}

\textbf{Equation:} Primary Benefits / Primary Costs (customizable via flags). Benefits: Congestion (optional) + Curtailment (optional) + Delivered energy. Costs: Capital + Delay (always) + O\&M, Insurance, Wildfire, Outage, Line Losses, Emissions (optional). If no flags are set, equals \texttt{bcr\_system}.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Wildfire Risk - Outage Risk)

\textbf{Perspective:} Societal perspective excluding both wildfire and outage risk costs.

\textbf{Includes:} Benefits: Congestion + Curtailment + Delivered energy (excludes revenue). Costs: Capital + Operational + Energy/Emissions + Delay (excludes wildfire + outage).

\textbf{Use Case:} Analysis when both wildfire and outage risk costs may be insured, managed separately, or contextually inappropriate.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Emissions Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Risk + Delay + Line Losses (excludes emissions only).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_linelosses}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Line Loss Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Risk + Delay + Emissions (excludes line losses only).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Emissions Costs - Line Loss Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Risk + Delay (excludes emissions + line losses).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Wildfire Risk - Outage Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Line Losses.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_linelosses\_and\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Line Losses - Wildfire Risk - Outage Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Emissions.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses\_and\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Line Losses - Wildfire Risk - Outage Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay only.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Wildfire Risk Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Energy/Emissions + Delay + Outage Risk (excludes wildfire).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_wildfire\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Wildfire Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Line Losses + Outage Risk.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_linelosses\_and\_wildfire\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Line Loss Costs - Wildfire Risk Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Emissions + Outage Risk.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses\_and\_wildfire\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Line Losses - Wildfire Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Outage Risk only.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits (haircut) / (Total Costs - Outage Risk Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Energy/Emissions + Delay + Wildfire Risk (excludes outage).

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Outage Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Line Losses + Wildfire Risk.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_linelosses\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Line Loss Costs - Outage Risk Costs). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Emissions + Wildfire Risk.

\subsection{\texttt{bcr\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses\_and\_outage\_risk}}

\textbf{Equation:} Total Benefits / (Total Costs - Emissions - Line Losses - Outage Risk). Costs: Capital + Operational + Delay + Wildfire Risk only.

\section{Net Benefits}

All BCRs have corresponding net benefit calculations (Present Value unless noted).

\subsection{System/Societal Net Benefits}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_pv} = Total Benefits (haircut) - Total Costs
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_nominal} = Total Benefits (nominal) - Total Costs (nominal), undiscounted
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_primary\_pv} = Primary Benefits - Primary Costs (customizable)
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk\_pv} = Total Benefits - (Total Costs - Wildfire Risk - Outage Risk)
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk\_pv}, \texttt{net\_benefit\_excluding\_outage\_risk\_pv}
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses\_pv}
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_excluding\_emissions\_and\_linelosses\_and\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk\_pv}
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_capital\_only\_pv}, \texttt{net\_benefit\_capital\_and\_delay\_pv}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Stakeholder Net Benefits}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_utility\_pv} = Revenue - (Capital + Delay + Operational Costs)
\item \texttt{net\_benefit\_ratepayer\_pv} = (Congestion + Curtailment + Delivered energy Benefits) - (Revenue + Line Loss Costs)
\end{itemize}

\section{Important Notes}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Haircut Benefits:} Most BCRs use conservative ``haircut'' benefits (saturation factors applied to congestion/curtailment).
\item \textbf{Present Value:} All values are in present value (PV) terms, discounted to base year.
\item \textbf{Context:} Wildfire costs assume California-style catastrophic events. For lower wildfire risk regions, \texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk} may be more appropriate. Outage risk is more universal. Line losses are socialized through rates; emissions are externalities.
\item \textbf{Separating Wildfire and Outage Risk:} Use \texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk} for Virginia-style scenarios; \texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk} when both should be excluded; \texttt{bcr\_excluding\_outage\_risk} when only outage risk should be excluded.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Example: Scenario 1 (Rural Overhead AC 657MW)}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{bcr\_utility = 0.36} $\to$ Utility loses money.
\item \texttt{bcr\_ratepayer = 0.22} $\to$ Ratepayers lose money.
\item \texttt{bcr\_system = 0.02} $\to$ Very poor societal return (wildfire risk dominates).
\item \texttt{bcr\_excluding\_wildfire\_risk\_and\_outage\_risk = 0.24} $\to$ Better but still negative.
\end{itemize}

The separation of wildfire and outage risk allows for more nuanced regional analysis.
